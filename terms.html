The app

Clap Alert helps you find your phone nearby. When you turn on Clap mode or Whistle mode, the app listens through the microphone for a clap pattern or a whistle. When it hears one, the phone plays a loud alert sound, vibrates and blinks its flashlight. You can choose the alert sound, volume, alert length, vibration, flashlight and sensitivity. Detection keeps running with the screen off, with an ongoing notification, until you tap Stop. The free version shows ads. Premium removes ads and unlocks all alert sounds and extra flash and vibration patterns.

Important: detection is not guaranteed

  • Detection depends on your phone's microphone, background noise, how far away you are, battery saver settings and the limits your phone's maker puts on apps running in the background.
  • The app can miss a clap or a whistle, and it can sometimes fire on a similar sound, such as music, a door or a dog bark.
  • Some phones stop background apps on their own. If this happens, detection stops until you open the app and turn it on again.
  • Clap Alert is not a security, anti theft or emergency tool. It does not find a phone by GPS or over the internet, and it cannot help if the phone is out of hearing range, switched off or out of battery. Do not rely on it to protect your phone, your safety or anyone else's.

Use it with care

Alerts can be very loud. Do not hold the phone near your ear while an alert may fire, and turn detection off where a sudden loud sound or flashing light would be unsafe or disturb others, for example while driving, in meetings, in hospitals or near people sensitive to flashing light. Detection uses the microphone and some battery while it is on.
