Common questions

It does not hear my clap.

Open Settings and raise the sensitivity. Clap 2 or 3 times, sharply and close together. Make sure Clap mode is on and the detection notification is showing. If it only fails with the screen off, turn battery saver off and allow Clap Alert to run in the background: in Android settings open Apps, Clap Alert, Battery, and choose Unrestricted. Some phone makers also have their own app sleeping or auto launch lists, so check that Clap Alert is allowed there too.

The alert goes off by itself.

Another sharp sound, such as music, a door or a dog bark, can sound like a clap. Lower the sensitivity in Settings, or switch to Whistle mode, which reacts to a steady whistle and fires less often by mistake.

How do I stop the alert?

Tap Stop on the alert screen or in the notification. To stop detection completely, tap Stop on the ongoing detection notification or turn the mode off in the app.

Does the app record what I say?

No. Sound is checked in real time in your phone's memory only to notice a clap or a whistle. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere, and the microphone is used only while you have detection turned on. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Why is there always a notification?

Android requires it while an app uses the microphone in the background. It shows you that detection is on and gives you a Stop button.

Can it find my phone if it is far away or lost outside?

No. Clap Alert only works when the phone is close enough to hear you. It does not use GPS and is not an anti theft or emergency tool.

How do I cancel the subscription?

Open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Clap Alert, Cancel subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for. If you cancel during the free trial, you are not charged.

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Google account you subscribed with, open Clap Alert, open Settings and tap Restore purchases.

Can I change my ad consent choice?

Yes. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, open the app's Settings and tap Ad privacy.
