Privacy Policy for Clap Alert

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Clap Alert (shown on Google Play as Clap Alert: Find My Phone) is an Android app that helps you find your phone: while Clap mode or Whistle mode is on, it listens for a clap or a whistle and then plays a loud alert, vibrates and blinks the flashlight. This page explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the app with package com.clapalert.findmyphone, published on Google Play by Billionweb.

The short version

  • Audio is never recorded. The microphone is used only to notice a clap or a whistle. Sound is checked in real time in the phone's memory, a few milliseconds at a time, and is then thrown away. It is never saved, never sent to us and never shared with anyone.
  • The microphone is on only when you turn detection on. While it is on, an ongoing notification shows it, and you can stop it at any time with the Stop button.
  • No speech recognition. The app does not listen to, understand or store what anyone says.
  • No camera, no photos, no files, no contacts, no location. The flashlight is switched on through Android's torch setting only. The app has no camera permission and never takes photos or video.
  • There is no account. You never sign in, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We use Google Firebase to count how the app is used and to receive crash reports, and Google AdMob to show ads in the free version. These receive device and usage information described below, and never any audio.
  • Subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before ads are personalised or your data is used for advertising, and you can change that choice at any time.

How the microphone is used

This is the most important part of this policy, so here it is in full.

  • When. The app uses the microphone only while you have Clap mode or Whistle mode turned on. When you tap Stop, in the app or in the notification, the microphone is released.
  • How. The app reads a few milliseconds of sound at a time and checks it, in memory on your phone, for the sharp pattern of a clap or the steady tone of a whistle. Each small piece of sound is discarded as soon as it has been checked.
  • What never happens. Audio is never recorded, never written to a file, never uploaded, never sent to us or to any third party, and never used for speech recognition or to identify anyone. Analytics, crash reports and ads never contain audio.
  • What you can see. While detection is on, Android shows an ongoing notification and its own microphone indicator, so you always know the microphone is in use.

Detection with the screen off

So that it can still hear you when the phone is locked, detection runs as an Android foreground service of the microphone type. This service always shows an ongoing notification with a Stop button. Detection keeps running until you tap Stop. It does not start by itself.

Information that stays on your device

Your alert settings (sound, volume, alert length, vibration, flashlight and sensitivity), your chosen mode and your language are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The alert history, a list of when alerts fired, holds only the date and time of each alert. None of this is stored on a server, sent to us or shared with anyone. If you contact support from the app, your own email app opens and you decide what to send us.

Advertising and your consent choices

The free version shows ads. Premium shows no ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalised and whether your data may be used for advertising and measurement. Until you choose, ads are not requested. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app's Settings under Ad privacy. Elsewhere, you can reset or delete your advertising identifier in Android settings under Privacy, Ads.

Subscriptions

Premium is a weekly subscription sold through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment and tells the app whether a subscription is active. We receive no card details, no billing address and no name. You manage or cancel the subscription in the Google Play Store under Payments and subscriptions.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the Google services listed above, which act on our behalf or under their own terms as described. These providers may process information outside your country under their own safeguards. Audio is never shared with anyone, because it never leaves your phone.

Retention

Settings and the alert history remain on your device until you clear them, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Audio is not retained at all. Google Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months. Crash reports are retained by Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Advertising data follows Google's retention practices.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings removes everything the app stored on your device, including the alert history. To ask us to delete analytics data associated with your installation, or to exercise any other privacy right you have under local law, email arshpreet@billionweb.co with the subject "Clap Alert data deletion". Because there is no account, we will explain what we can locate and what limited information, if any, we need. Never send passwords or identity documents.

Children

Clap Alert is a general audience app for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data sent to Google services travels over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we use the protections these services provide.
